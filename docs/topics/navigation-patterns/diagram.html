1. Direct Navigation Coupling (Anti-Pattern)

Source View directly initializes Destination View and calls navigationController.pushViewController. This tightly couples the two screens and prevents Source View from being reused in different flows.

// Tightly coupled\nlet detail = DetailViewController(id: 42)\nnavigationController?.pushViewController(detail, animated: true)
Step 1: Direct Coupling: View controllers instantiate and push each other directly, preventing reusability and clean deep linking.
Step 2: Flow Delegation: The source view informs its Coordinator via a delegate or closure rather than pushing a view controller.
Step 3: Coordinator Push: The Coordinator constructs the destination view with its dependencies and coordinates presentation.
Step 4: SwiftUI Router: In modern SwiftUI, an observable Router manages a NavigationStack path array, driving transitions declaratively.
