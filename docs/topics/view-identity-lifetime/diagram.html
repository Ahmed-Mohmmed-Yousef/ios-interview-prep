STABLE IDENTITY
Step 1 of 4
SwiftUI views are lightweight structs created on every render. However, SwiftUI links a persistent identity node to internal @State storage as long as the view position remains stable. 
// Single stable identity:
CardView()
    .background(isSelected ? .blue : .gray) // Modifies property without changing identity
  1. Step 1: Stable Identity with Ternary Modifier — A single view node in the hierarchy preserves identity and persistent @State storage.
  2. Step 2: Property Changes Animate Smoothly — Property updates on a stable view preserve @State and animate seamlessly.
  3. Step 3: Structural Branching with if/else — An if/else statement creates two distinct structural positions in the view tree.
  4. Step 4: Branch Swap Resets Lifetime — Switching branches terminates the previous identity, discarding old @State and initializing fresh state.
