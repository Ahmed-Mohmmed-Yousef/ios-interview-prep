Step 1: App Registration Request

Step 1 of 4
Handshake Initiated
The app calls registerForRemoteNotifications(). iOS establishes a secure connection to APNs.
UIApplication.shared.registerForRemoteNotifications()
  1. Step 1 (Register): The app requests authorization and invokes UIApplication.shared.registerForRemoteNotifications().
  2. Step 2 (Token Issued): APNs generates an opaque device token and passes it to the app in didRegisterForRemoteNotificationsWithDeviceToken.
  3. Step 3 (Token Upload): The app converts the binary token to hexadecimal and uploads it to the backend customer database.
  4. Step 4 (Push Delivery): When an event occurs, the backend sends a JSON payload to APNs via HTTP/2, and APNs delivers the notification banner to the device.
