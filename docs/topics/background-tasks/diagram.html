Request Queued
The app registers permitted identifiers in Info.plist and submits a BGAppRefreshTaskRequest with an earliest begin date.
BGTaskScheduler.shared.submit(refreshRequest)
  1. Step 1 (Submit Request): App submits a BGTaskRequest to BGTaskScheduler specifying an earliest begin date.
  2. Step 2 (System Monitoring): App enters suspended state. iOS monitors battery status, charging state, network connectivity, and user habits.
  3. Step 3 (Opportunistic Launch): When conditions are optimal, iOS wakes the app in the background and executes the registered launch handler.
  4. Step 4 (Complete & Resuspend): App sets expirationHandler, executes async work, calls setTaskCompleted(success: true), and returns to suspended state.
