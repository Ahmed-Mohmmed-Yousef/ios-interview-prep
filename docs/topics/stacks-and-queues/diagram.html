Stack: Push Items Step 1

Items "A" and "B" pushed to the stack. "B" sits at the top. Queue holds "1" at front and "2" at back.

Stack Push Operation Step 1 of 4

var stack = ["A"] stack.append("B") // push "B" onto top // Top element is now "B"

Elements are added to the top of the stack. Last element inserted becomes the first element accessible.

StructureStack
ActionPush
TimeO(1)

Step 1: Stack Push (LIFO)

Pushing elements places each new item on top of the vertical stack. Most recently added item is immediately accessible.

Step 2: Stack Pop (Last-In, First-Out)

Popping removes the top item in O(1) time. Item "B" leaves first, exposing "A" as the new top.

Step 3: Queue Enqueue (FIFO)

Items enter the queue at the Back pointer. "1" is at the front, followed by "2" at the back.

Step 4: Queue Dequeue (First-In, First-Out)

Dequeuing removes the oldest element from the Front in O(1) time. "1" exits first, advancing Front to "2".
