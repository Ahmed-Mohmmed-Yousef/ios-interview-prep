iOS Interview Path Map

The draft road for each level, from fresh to staff. Levels add up: a senior learner walks the fresh, junior, mid and senior stages in order. Tap a topic to see its goal and what it needs.

Numbers show the order on the main road. A topic opens when the one before it is passed.

Bonus topics sit on a side branch. They are optional and do not count in the score.

Needs lists the topics a lesson builds on. A dashed chip means the lesson gives a short intro to a topic that comes later.

Sources are checked links. Most are official Apple or Swift pages; Community marks trusted non-Apple sources, allowed only where Apple has no page.
