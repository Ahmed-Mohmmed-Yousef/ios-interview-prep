## Scope decisions (from the user)

- **No Objective-C.** Swift only.
- **Apple tools only.** Popular third-party libraries (for example Alamofire, RxSwift, TCA) are only mentioned briefly inside topics: what they are and when teams use them.
- **iPhone and iPad.** No watchOS, visionOS or macOS topics.

## How to read the evidence

- **Official** = swift.org, docs.swift.org, the Swift book repo, developer.apple.com (docs, WWDC guides). Facts about Swift and iOS come from these.
- **Interview sources** = prep sites, guides and candidate reports. They tell us *what is asked*, not *what is true*. Lower confidence; used only to choose topics and levels.

Levels in `topic-candidates.md` are **suggestions**. Step 1.4 groups topics into units, step 1.5 sets the final levels with you.
