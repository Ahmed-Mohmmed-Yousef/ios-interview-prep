Phase 3 · Step 3.1 · Screens and flows

How learners move through the app

Twelve screens and eight flows. The main loop is simple: path, lesson, exam, result, next stop.

Your decisions

Skipped topics in the totalCount with the skip-test score
Answer optionsShuffled every time
A passed topic is updatedBadge and note; the pass stays
Locked topicsFully locked until the one before is done
Changing levelAny time; progress kept
First visitShort welcome, then pick a level

Words and numbers

Topic scoreThe best exam score for a topic. A topic passes at 70%.
Skipped topicA topic covered by a passed skip test (80%). Its score is the skip-test score.
ProgressDone topics divided by all main topics on the path. Done means passed or skipped.
Path scoreThe average score of all done main topics. At the end of the path, this is the total result.
Bonus topicsOptional side roads. They count in neither number.
Skip testsOnly for stages below your level. A senior can skip fresh, junior or mid topics, by stage or by unit.
